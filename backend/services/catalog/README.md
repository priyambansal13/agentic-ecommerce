# catalog service

Shared catalog (one product page, many seller **offers**), categories with **gating**, the **Brand Registry** (applications, brands, authorized resellers) and listing rules: verified seller, approved gated category, only the brand owner creates products of a registered brand, restricted brands need owner authorization to resell, price ≤ MRP, one offer per seller and product (422 `ListingRejection` codes). New products start PENDING_REVIEW and are published/rejected by `admin.moderation_decided.v1`. Local read model `seller_eligibility` from `user.events`. APIs: public `GET /v1/categories`, `GET /v1/products?q&category&page`, `GET /v1/products/:id` · seller `POST /v1/products`, `POST /v1/offers`, `GET /v1/sellers/me/listings`, `GET /v1/sellers/me/eligibility`, `POST /v1/brands/applications`, `GET /v1/brands/mine`, `PUT|DELETE /v1/brands/:brandId/resellers/:sellerId` (owner; revoking suppresses that reseller's offers) · brand-team `GET /v1/brand-applications/queue`, `POST /v1/brand-applications/:id/decision`. Events: `catalog.product_submitted.v1`, `catalog.offer_created.v1`, `catalog.brand_approved.v1`. Dashboards: `GET /v1/dashboard` (admin, moderator — listing counts). **Product images** (own bucket `ac-catalog`): public `GET /v1/images/:imageId/:size` (WebP thumb 160 / card 400 / full 1000 px, immutable cache), `GET /v1/products/:id/image/:size` and `GET /v1/categories/:id/image/:size` (302 to the image) · seller `POST /v1/products/:id/images` (multipart `file`, JPEG/PNG/WebP ≤ 5 MB, ≥ 500 px, max 5; creator or registered brand owner; 403/413/422 `ImageRejection`), `DELETE /v1/products/:id/images/:imageId`, `PUT /v1/products/:id/images/order`. Uploads are sniffed by magic bytes, re-encoded and stripped of metadata (EXIF/GPS). Demo photos: curated CC0 / public-domain / CC BY stock photos (`@ac/demo-data` `images.json`, credited via `attribution`) stored once under `stock/<key>/`; seller uploads under `products/<productId>/<imageId>/`. Offer re-pricing for cart/checkout: public `GET /v1/offers?ids=` (≤ 50; `active` = offer ACTIVE and product PUBLISHED); sellers change price or pause with `PATCH /v1/offers/:id`; every price/active change (incl. moderation and reseller revocation) emits `catalog.offer_updated.v1`.
